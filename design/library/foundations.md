# Foundations

FamilyHub separates three appearance axes:

- **Preset:** a versioned token family such as `current` or `morgenruhe`.
- **Scheme:** `light`, `dark`, or the reduced-stimulation `night` mode.
- **Presentation:** `standard` or touch-oriented `wall` display.

Semantic colors, typography, radii, motion, shadows, and touch targets live in `src/theme/`. Feature components consume theme values rather than literal design values.
